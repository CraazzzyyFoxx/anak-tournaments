### Four games on one platform

Tournaments and mixes now run in League of Legends, Deadlock and VALORANT, not just Overwatch. The switcher in the header shows every game at once or just one, and lists show which game each tournament and mix is played in.

You can sign in with Steam. Link it and your Deadlock rank shows on your profile and keeps itself up to date.

The games don't all do the same things yet: detailed player statistics, achievements and analytics are most complete for Overwatch, and VALORANT ranks are entered by organizers.

### Mixes: every lobby gets its own room

A mix can run up to six lobbies, each with its own room: lineups with captains, the match start, the result and the history. Anyone can watch a room, signed out and on a phone.

Teams come from the balancer or a live captain draft. Before a match captains take turns banning heroes, and in League of Legends and Deadlock they pick them too. A captain gets a Discord message when the draft starts or bans open.

### All-time statistics

The Statistics page is now a tournament almanac: headline totals, a player ranking you can search by name, records, and with a game picked, the most played heroes and maps.

<Shot src="/changelog/2.0.0/almanac.en.webp" width={2760} height={1520} alt="The tournament almanac: headline totals and the player ranking by titles" />

In “Who plays it best?” pick a hero to see the top 10 and a table of everyone who played them. Players with fewer than five games on that hero are hidden by default.

#### For players

- Steam accounts show the profile name instead of a long number.
- Deadlock and VALORANT have no role picker: each has a single role.
- Percentages are shown unrounded: 25.2% no longer appears as 25.0%.
- A player profile loads 5-30 times less data.
- Tournament analytics uses the site's common design and no longer scrolls sideways on phones.

</div>
<div>

#### For organizers

- A captain or organizer loads a Deadlock match by its ID, and the result and stats come with it.
- In League of Legends captains pick a side in the pick-ban room.
- A player's tournament rank can be left empty, for example in 1v1 duels.
- Achievements are counted separately for each game.
- Community owners turn games on and set their order themselves.
- A new community is set up in steps, and everything after the first step can be skipped.
