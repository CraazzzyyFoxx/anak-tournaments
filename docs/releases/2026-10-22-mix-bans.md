# Mix rooms, stage П1 — hero bans and LoL side pick: release note

A mix can play its matches with hero bans, or with a side pick in LoL. The host picks a ban preset in the mix header:
one of the game's presets that a mix may play (the rules catalog marks them `mix_eligible`), or none. Viewers see the
choice read-only. Each game has a default in the host's account settings, which a new mix copies; a cloned mix keeps
its source's. Start match seats the team that bans first in slot 1: the loser of the lobby's last recorded match when
the same two teams meet again, otherwise (or after a draw) the team with the lower average rating. The host may swap
it. Start also freezes the preset on the lobby, so a header edit applies from the next Start. The lobby is then
**Pre-game** until its hero ban room completes or is cancelled, and **Live** after that. Captains ban on their turns.
The mix's host and co-hosts can act for either team and pause, extend, reset or cancel the room. A turn that times out
is filled at random, and anyone can watch, signed out included. Every started match's room now has a chat that only
its seated players and the hosts can read. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-21-mix-rooms-draft-bans-design.md` §8.

Two migrations ship, in order after `mixaway01`. **`mixban01`** adds `mix.custom_game.hero_ban_preset` (nullable) and
`balancer.user_config.hero_ban_presets` (JSONB, default `{}`). **`mixban02`** adds `mix.lobby.live_ban_preset`
(nullable). Both only add columns, with no backfill. Run them, then deploy everything **from one SHA**, in this order:

1. **tournament-service** first. It declares the consumer queue `tournament_pick_ban_room_open` (bound to
   `tournament.events` with `tournament.encounter.pick_ban_room_open_requested`) and its DLQ. A queue that has not been
   declared does not buffer. A Start published before this deploy loses its room-open request, and that lobby stays
   in Pre-game with no ban board until a host presses Cancel match.
2. **balancer-service**, the producer. It writes the request to the outbox in Start's transaction.
3. The **gateway** (route, ACL, regenerated `schemas.json`) and the **frontend**. The prefs PUT now requires
   `hero_ban_presets`, so the previous frontend cannot save account settings until the new one is live.

Without a preset, a match plays as before with two differences: Start marks both participations ready, and the room
has its chat.

## Wire changes

| Change | Kind |
| --- | --- |
| `PUT …/custom-games/{id}/ban-preset` (`rpc.balancer.custom.set_ban_preset`, body `{hero_ban_preset}`, hosts only; 422 `unknown_ban_preset`) | new |
| Mix `hero_ban_preset`; lobby `state: pregame`, `live_seats`, `opener_index`; history round `bans` (`[{slot, item_id}]`, in the order they were made) | additive |
| `POST …/match/start` body `flip_opener` (default false) | additive |
| Prefs (`/api/v1/balancer/me/mix-preferences`) `hero_ban_presets`: returned by GET, **required** on PUT | **breaking** for old clients of the PUT |
| Rules catalog (`rpc.tournament.pick_ban_rules_catalog`) preset `mix_eligible` | additive |
| History `teams[]`: slot 1 is the team that banned first (the lineup's first team without bans) | changed meaning |
| Pick-ban, readiness and chat RPCs load mix encounters; the room's organizer RPCs accept the mix's host and co-hosts | widened |
| Gateway: a mix match's `encounter:<id>:pick-ban:hero` is public; its `encounter:<id>:chat` is only for its seated players and hosts | widened |
| Chat role `player` (a mix's seated non-captain) | additive |
| Event `tournament.encounter.pick_ban_room_open_requested` (`PickBanRoomOpenRequestedEvent`) | new |
| `workspace.pickup_mix` invalidation (`change: "pick_ban"`) when a mix's hero room completes, is cancelled or comes back | additive |

## Not in this release

Per-player (target) bans in mixes: a preset's per-player steps on later maps never apply, because every mix match is
map 1 of a Bo1. A "bans n/N" counter on lobby cards: in Pre-game a card shows the elapsed time. Also not here: the
captain draft (П2), Discord DMs and card states (П3), and hero picks (П4).
